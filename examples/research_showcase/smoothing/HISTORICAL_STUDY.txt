Metrics and numerical scope:
O1 is EE arc length in metres. O2 integrates max(0,1-world_clearance/0.1)^2 over weighted joint arc. O3 integrates visibility loss over the same arc using the existing 64-ray mounted-camera model.
Raw costs are re-evaluated too; differences from stored PRM quadrature are numerical integration differences, not objective-definition changes.
Geometric roughness: equal UNWEIGHTED joint-arc resampling at N=256; RMS norm of second finite differences divided by (1/(N-1))^2. The 512-sample result is also reported. This is resolution-dependent geometric roughness, not physical acceleration or jerk.
Corners in joint space are measured in degrees of direction change between consecutive displacement vectors on the same 512-sample grid; these are not joint-angle magnitudes.
Cost convergence guard for selected paths: C_new+estimated_error <= (1+eta)*(C_original-estimated_error), with 1e-10 roundoff slack. Estimated error is the absolute difference from nested half-resolution quadrature, not a rigorous integral enclosure.
All candidate curves: continuous Bezier convex-hull clearance checks. Final selections: a separate Bezier-to-chord displacement bound with Python edge_reference at inflated margin, plus refined on-curve cost evaluation.
SAFE representatives also retain sampled world minimum within 0.1mm. Other representatives retain the hard >10mm constraint but can trade bottleneck clearance against integrated costs.
The current collision pair exclusions and conservative robot/camera assembly remain the existing model. No physical execution occurred.
New nondominated counts are candidate-pool results, not proven global Pareto solutions. Both-resolution membership is provided to expose numerical sensitivity. Count does not equal number of motion families.
A smaller augmented set can mean improved objective coverage: new candidate curves dominate old points. No original files are removed.
Smoothing also reduces pairwise geometric separation. Consult diversity_before_after.csv; preserving objective budgets does not guarantee preserving motion families.
New curves can improve all objectives of a graph path because they leave the finite roadmap edge set. This does not contradict graph-optimality.
Cubic B-splines/natural cubic paths are C2 in their spline parameter; quadratic local corner blends are C1 geometrically; plain shortcuts remain piecewise linear.
No Ruckig/TOPPRA, hardware timing, actuator feasibility or expensive nonlinear trajectory optimizer was run.

All 1063 protected input files retain their original SHA-256.
Saved curve representation: NPZ bezier[segment, 4 control points, 6 joints], cubic Bernstein parameter t in [0,1] per segment. Do not execute the control points as waypoints. Segment parameter durations are not execution timestamps.
Default visual comparison uses eta=0. Tables include eta=0, 0.01, 0.05 and every tested candidate. Original and new paths remain separate.
